The City Rings: Together Across Soundscapes

Our client Aifoon is a research organisation focused on sound. They help children learn how to listen with their project ‘The City Rings’ where primary school children from 2 different countries world-wide record the sounds of their cities and share them with each other. Our solution is a plug-and-play online platform that replaces, simplifies and improves all the tools that are used for the project. Using a story-like format we will allow the children to become co-creators in this experience.
